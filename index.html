Daniel Madi

AI, infrastructure and security engineering

I'm an engineer in Lebanon. I build AI systems that run in production: voice and messaging agents, the integrations that connect them to business software, and the servers and data layers underneath.

I co-founded Meta Empires, where I lead engineering on Hala AI, a multi-tenant platform whose agents answer calls and messages across phone, WhatsApp, Instagram and the web, and book appointments or update CRM records mid-conversation. I also work as a Technical Integration Engineer at Layout Intl, on client integrations, Linux server operations and incident response.

Security runs through all of it. I'm completing a postgraduate diploma in cybersecurity at the American University of Beirut, hold ISC2 and Fortinet certifications, and write up the security findings I report.
