Projects

Hala AI
Hala AI is an end-to-end platform for AI-driven business communication. It handles customer conversations across web, WhatsApp, and other modern channels — qualifying leads, answering questions, routing inquiries, and booking appointments without human intervention. Built with a focus on reliability and real operational value, Hala replaces fragmented manual workflows with intelligent, always-on agents that integrate directly into how a business runs. The goal is not to bolt AI onto existing processes, but to redesign those processes around what AI can now do well.
